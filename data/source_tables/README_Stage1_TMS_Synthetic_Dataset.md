**What the brief does NOT specify:** no dataset, fields, data types, row count, volume, data-modelling approach, or tool list. Everything about the dataset below is therefore my assumption.

Why: it is the only dataset shape the public site can credibly anchor (real price points, real product lines, real shipping/payment rules), and an orders fact table (header + line items + product dimension) is a natural workload for HDFS/Hive/Spark/MapReduce experiments.

**Could not verify / conflicts**
- **No real sales data is public.** Order volumes, AOV, basket mix, channel split, payment mix, repeat rate, seasonality and cancellation rate are all my assumptions.
- **Price staleness:** snapshots in the catalog are roughly 150 to 1,000 days old (`snapshot_age_days`); only 75 catalog rows come from the live fetch. Different snapshots disagree, for example Pharaoh Neo massage chair 18,800 / 25,100 / 30,000; Magnificence mattress 84,225 vs 127,400; Mini Gun massager 400 vs 500; Holburn Euro Top 750 vs 1,000. - Shipping page contradicts itself: "free regardless of order value" versus AED 50 same-day under AED 300 and AED 299/599 express fees (I treated the fees as optional premium services). Express coverage for Umm Al Quwain and Fujairah is not stated (I assumed standard delivery only).

## 6. Assumptions I made (all adjustable in `generate_orders.py`)

1. **Row count 10,000 orders** (about 14.7k lines): brief gives none. Scale with `--orders`, e.g. for big-data benchmarks.
2. **Window:** the last 12 full months, 2025-11-01 to 2026-10-31.
3. **Basket mix** (share of orders): bedding-only 33%, value mattress 37.2% (+ attach items), toppers 3%, luxury Vispring mattress 0.8%, adjustable bed 3.5%, massager 3.5%, massage chair 0.8%, sofa 7.5%, recliner/chair 4%, nightstand 2.5%, tables/dining 2.5%, bed frame 0.5%, decor 1.2%. Invented; there is no public mix.
4. **Size ladder** for Englander mattresses derived from one observed product, with a popularity-weighted size choice (180x200 most common).
5. **Markdowns:** a line with an observed markdown sells at the sale price 60% of the time and at regular price otherwise.
6. **Channel** 58% online / 42% showroom; emirate, payment, delivery-option, repeat-rate (14%), cancellation (2.0%) and defect-return (0.7%) rates are plausible guesses. BNPL probability rises with basket value.
7. **Seasonality** (monthly weights, weekday/hour shapes, mall hours 10:00-22:00) is generic UAE retail seasonality, not observed TMS data.
8. VAT 5% inclusive, also on delivery fees; express/same-day fees are paid on top of items.
9. Tiering follows the site's own value/luxury split; "mid" is my label.
